2

Approvers are flying blind — no context, no speed

Real-world problem · Approver bottleneck & decision quality

The problem

Only 3 approvers handle 1,000+ daily claims, each requiring them to manually open an email, locate the vehicle record, check warranty coverage, cross-reference service history, and form a judgment — with no AI assistance. Average review time is 15–20 minutes per claim. High-value or ambiguous claims get delayed, dealers chase status by phone, and approvers burn out.

Agentforce solution

Instant notification via Platform Event the moment a claim is submitted, with an Agentforce Approver Assistant that surfaces a pre-built AI summary directly inside a Slack message — covering vehicle history, warranty eligibility, part cost estimate, and a recommended action. The approver reads a 30-second brief instead of 15 minutes of manual research. Data Cloud enriches every summary with telematics and prior claim patterns.

3

Back-and-forth follow-up loops stall every complex claim

Real-world problem · Resolution cycle time & fraud risk

The problem

For borderline or high-value claims, approvers email dealers with follow-up questions and wait hours or days for responses — creating a multi-round loop that stretches resolution from same-day to 3–5 business days. Approvers also have no systematic way to detect anomalies: duplicate claims, mileage fraud, or parts claimed outside covered failure modes. Every delay costs the OEM in dealer satisfaction and the dealer in repair bay downtime.

Agentforce solution

An Intelligent Assistant agent embedded in the Slack approval thread lets the approver ask natural language questions ("Has this dealer submitted claims for this part before?"). The agent reasons across Data Cloud, Automotive Cloud records, and warranty policy documents in real time — citing evidence for its answers. The Slack Approval Workflow then lets the approver approve, reject, or request dealer clarification with a single button, triggering a Salesforce Flow that notifies the dealer instantly via WhatsApp or web chat.
