Dealer warranty volume & approval standards

Document: WC-WTY-DLR-001  |  Revision: 2.0  |  Effective: April 2026  |  Audience: Internal warranty & dealer programs  |  Classification: Internal use only

Rolling periods are measured backward from today. Counts and rates apply per authorized dealer (account). Adjust thresholds with Warranty Program Office approval; supersede with the system of record if figures differ.

1. Claim volume per dealer (rolling windows)

Maximum submitted warranty claims allowed per dealer in each rolling period. Exceeding a limit triggers review (not an automatic stop); program office may grant exceptions.

Rolling period Maximum submitted claims per dealer
30 days15
60 days28
90 days40

2. Acceptable claim approval rate (per dealer)

Definition: Of all claims the dealer submitted in the period that reached a final decision, the share that were approved (excluding withdrawn or cancelled before decision).

Metric Acceptable range Follow-up
Approval rate (rolling 90 days) 75% to 92% Below the range: review quality of submissions. Above the range: confirm controls are not overly permissive.

Program office may set tighter targets for specific regions or product lines.
